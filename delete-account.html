Account and Data Deletion

LocalLog.in respects your privacy and your right to request deletion of your account.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Contact the LocalLog.in support team using the official contact details published on our website.
  2. Tell us that you want to delete your LocalLog.in account.
  3. Provide only the information reasonably necessary to identify your account.
  4. We will verify the request and explain the next steps.

Important: A working support contact and an actual deletion process must be established before this page is published as an active service.

What Happens to Your Data?

Once a deletion request is verified and processed, account information and associated personal data will be deleted where applicable, subject to legal obligations and legitimate security or dispute-resolution needs.

Any data that must be retained under applicable law will be retained only as required. The final deletion process should clearly explain what is deleted and what, if anything, is retained.
