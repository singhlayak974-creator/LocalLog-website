Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

LocalLog.in is a hyperlocal platform designed to help people connect with local workers and nearby service opportunities. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use our app or website.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, LocalLog.in may process account information such as your name, email address, profile details, skills, posts, work requests, offers, and chat messages.

If you grant location permission, the app may access location information to help identify nearby posts and opportunities. Location access can be controlled through your device settings.

Google sign-in and Firebase services may process information needed to authenticate users and provide app functionality.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display local work requests and service opportunities.
  • Connect people offering help with people requesting help.
  • Support offers, accepted connections, and chat features.
  • Maintain security and improve app reliability.

3. Information Visibility

Posts and information shared publicly in the app may be visible to other users. Information shared through offers and chats may be visible to the relevant participants.

Do not include sensitive personal information in public posts or messages unless necessary and appropriate.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

We may rely on third-party services, including Google and Firebase, for sign-in, data storage, and app functionality. These providers may process information as needed to provide their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We do not intend to sell users' personal information.

5. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users should be able to request account and associated data deletion. The process and any applicable exceptions must be explained on our Account Deletion page.

7. Children's Privacy

LocalLog.in is not intended to collect children's personal information in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the LocalLog.in team using the official support contact published on our website. A working support contact must be added before publication.
